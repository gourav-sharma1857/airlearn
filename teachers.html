For teachers

A ten-minute activity your students can hold

%BRAND_NAME% is a browser-based activity designed to help students understand how surface area and volume scale. Students grab a 3D cube with a pinch, stretch the edge from 2 to 4, and see surface area go from 24 to 96 (×4) and volume from 8 to 64 (×8).

What a session looks like

  • Predict. Students guess what happens to volume when the edge doubles.
  • Explore. They stretch the cube with their hand (or mouse) and watch the numbers.
  • Revisit. They answer the same question again and compare with their first guess.
  • Discuss. A short recap links the result to a² and a³.

What you need

  • A Chromebook or laptop with Chrome (recent version).
  • A webcam is optional: mouse and touch mode give the same demo.
  • No student accounts, no logins, no installs.
  • Video is processed on the device only and never uploaded. Details.

This is an early prototype. It covers one shape (the cube) and one idea (scaling of surface area and volume).
