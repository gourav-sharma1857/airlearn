Privacy

Your camera, your device.

Here is exactly what happens when you use %BRAND_NAME%, in plain language.

The short version

  • Hand tracking runs inside your browser, on your device.
  • We never record, store or upload video or pictures from your camera.
  • No accounts, no names, no logins for students.
  • You can stop the camera at any time, or skip it and use the mouse.

What the camera is used for

When you allow the camera, your browser shows the video on your screen. Our code looks at each video frame to find 21 points on your hand (like your fingertips and knuckles). It keeps only those points, uses them to move the cube, and then throws the frame away. This all happens in the browser on your computer. No video, pictures or hand points are sent over the internet.

We also check how bright the picture is, so we can say "try more light" if it's too dark. That is a single number and it also stays on your device.

What gets downloaded

To find your hand, the page downloads a small hand-tracking model (about 8 MB) and some code from this website. Downloads only go to your device. Nothing from your camera goes back.

What we count

To learn whether the tool is useful, we may count a few anonymous events:

  • that a page was opened,
  • that the camera was allowed,
  • how many times the cube or sphere was grabbed,
  • which Sphere Lab modes were opened (Fill, Paint, Cut, Challenge),
  • how many seconds the page was open.

These counts don't include your name, an ID, cookies, or anything from the camera.

Your answers

The lesson asks you to guess what happens to the volume. Your guesses are saved only in this browser (in "local storage") so the page can show your first guess next to your second. They are never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Teacher pilot signup

If a teacher fills in the pilot signup form, the details they type (like name, school email and school) are sent through our form provider, Formspree, so we can reply. We only use them to contact you about the pilot. Ask us any time to delete them.

Fonts

Text on this site uses Google Fonts, so your browser loads font files from Google's servers. No camera data is involved.

For the technically curious

Hand tracking uses Google's open-source MediaPipe Hand Landmarker, running in a Web Worker in your browser. The code that handles the camera is marked with a privacy note, and the site is set up to block uploads to any server other than the form and counting services listed above.
