After the demo

If you double the edge, what happens to the volume?

Your answer

Doubling the edge makes the volume 8 times as big, because the edge is used three times (length × width × height). Surface area only uses it twice, so it grows 4 times.

Recap

  • S ×4

    Surface area depends on the edge squared, so doubling the edge gives 2² = 4 times the area.

  • V ×8

    Volume depends on the edge cubed, so doubling the edge gives 2³ = 8 times the volume.

  • ×k

    The general rule: scale every length by k, and area scales by k², volume by k³.
