The big ideas

Fill it

Volume is the space inside, in cm³ (1 cm³ = 1 mL). Halfway up is exactly 50% full, but a quarter of the way up is only about 15.6%, because the bottom is narrow.

Paint it

Surface area is the outside skin, in cm². A painted band's area is 2πr × (how far down), so each centimetre of sweep takes the same paint.

Cut it

Cutting doesn't change the volume, but it adds two flat faces of πr² each, so surface area goes up 50%.

Double r

Scale every length by k, and volume grows by k³ while surface area grows by k². That's why the water wins.

The paint rate (1 mL per 10 cm²) is a teaching constant, not real paint data. The water example assumes an ideal thin-walled sphere.
