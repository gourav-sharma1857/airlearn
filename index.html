How it works

Four steps, about ten minutes, one big idea: when a shape grows, area and volume don't grow at the same speed.

  1. Open it

    Open the link in Chrome on a laptop or Chromebook. Nothing to install, no account.

  2. Allow the camera

    The camera only finds your hand. The video stays on your device and is never saved or sent.

  3. Pinch to grab

    Touch your thumb to your index finger to grab the cube. Move to spin it.

  4. Stretch and watch

    Pull the yellow corner. Edge, surface area and volume update live as you move.

Your video never leaves your device

Hand tracking runs inside your browser. We never record, store or upload video or pictures, and we don't ask for names or accounts. You can stop the camera at any time, or skip it and use the mouse.
